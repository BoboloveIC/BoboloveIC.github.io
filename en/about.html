Nationally Certified Counselor | Years of Clinical Experience

Hello, and thank you for being here. As a professional counselor, I understand how difficult it can be to take the first step toward seeking help. Whether you are struggling with anxiety, depression, relationship issues, or hoping to understand yourself better, I am here to accompany you on this journey.

I use an integrative approach, combining person-centered therapy, cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), mindfulness, and other modalities, tailoring the approach to each client's unique needs. I believe that everyone has the inner capacity for healing and growth, and my role is to help you discover and release that power.
