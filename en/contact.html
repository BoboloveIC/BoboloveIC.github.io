Frequently Asked Questions

No special preparation is needed. You may want to think about topics or concerns you'd like to discuss, but it's perfectly fine if you're not sure. The counselor will guide the initial conversation. Dress comfortably and arrive 5-10 minutes early to give yourself a relaxing transition.

A single session is usually 50 minutes (80 minutes for couples/family therapy). The overall duration varies: short-term counseling may be 8-12 sessions, medium-term about 20-30 sessions, and longer-term exploratory therapy may continue beyond that. Your counselor will discuss the expected timeline after the initial assessment.

Online and in-person therapy are professionally equivalent — extensive research has confirmed the effectiveness of online counseling. The main differences: in-person sessions offer a more immersive space with richer non-verbal cues; online sessions are more flexible and convenient, eliminating commute time, ideal for those with busy schedules or in remote locations.

If you've been experiencing emotional distress for more than two weeks that affects your daily life, work, or relationships — or if you want to understand yourself better and pursue personal growth — counseling may be helpful. Seeking counseling doesn't mean something is "wrong" with you; it shows you care about your mental health.

Please contact us directly by phone or WeChat for current fee information. The initial 30-minute assessment is free to help you determine if counseling is right for you. Currently, counseling services are not covered by medical insurance.

Important Information

1 Counseling is voluntary — clients should have a genuine desire to seek help.
2 The therapeutic relationship is professional. Counselors maintain clear professional boundaries.
3 All session content is strictly confidential, except when required by law (risk of self-harm, harm to others, etc.).
4 Appointments require advance booking. Please cancel at least 24 hours in advance. No-shows may affect future bookings.
5 Counseling is a gradual process requiring active participation and patience. Change doesn't happen overnight.
6 Minors require a guardian to accompany them and sign an informed consent form.
7 If your needs exceed the counselor's scope of practice, a referral will be responsibly provided.
8 Sessions are billed per appointment. Uncanceled no-shows will be charged the standard fee.
