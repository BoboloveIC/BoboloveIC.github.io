Service Options

Choose the approach that best fits your needs

🧠

Individual Counseling

50 minutes / session

One-on-one in-depth therapy to help you explore inner struggles and find pathways to growth. In a safe, confidential environment, we can work through:

  • Anxiety and stress management
  • Depression and mood improvement
  • Relationship issues and social anxiety
  • Self-awareness and personal growth
  • Trauma recovery
  • Career development and life planning
🎓

Youth Counseling

50 minutes / session

Specialized support for adolescents aged 12-18, tailored to their developmental stage. Addressing the unique psychological needs of young people:

  • Academic pressure and exam anxiety
  • Emotional regulation
  • Parent-child communication and family relationships
  • Peer relationships and school adjustment
  • Identity and self-exploration
  • Internet addiction and behavioral issues
👪

Couples & Family Therapy

80 minutes / session

Helping couples and family members improve communication, resolve conflicts, and rebuild emotional connections. Facing relational challenges together:

  • Communication and conflict resolution
  • Intimacy repair and rebuilding
  • Parent-child relationship improvement
  • Family roles and boundary setting
  • Major life transition adjustment
  • Marital crisis intervention
💻

Online Counseling

50 minutes / session

Secure, encrypted video platform for remote therapy without geographical limits. Online therapy is equally effective:

  • No commute, flexible scheduling
  • Encrypted video platform for privacy
  • Same professional quality as in-person
  • Ideal for those with limited mobility or remote locations
  • Quiet, private space required
  • Stable internet connection needed
